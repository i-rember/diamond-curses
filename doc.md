## Main Game

### ◆1-X - A Dance of Fire and Ice but it's excruciatingly long
Every outside-angled tile has a 2-beat pause.

### ◆2-X - Offbeats but the vertical sections are very short
All vertical straight tiles are removed.

### ◆3-X - THE WIND-UP but it's very wound up
Inside-angled tiles are changed to be halfway between their original angle and 0°.
Outside-angled tiles are adjusted halfway toward 360°.
Straight tiles are unchanged.

### ◆4-X - Love Letters but they're actually hate letters
Twirls are placed and removed such that all angles are inside, switching to outside and back after every segment of straight tiles.
- Inverse: The switch happens on every non-straight tile.

### ◆5-X - The Midnight Train but it's slightly longer
Every section of straight tiles has an extra straight tile appended to it.

### ◆6-X - PULSE but it was designed strangely
The chart is moved one tile backwards, and speed changes are added to undo this effect.
- Inverse: the chart is moved the other way.

### ◆7-X - Spin 2 Win but there are no icons
All holds, twirls, and speed changes are removed (unless they were already hidden.)

### ◆8-X - Jungle City but there's no repeat tiles
Any tiles that have the same angle as the previous tile are removed.

### ◆9-X - Classic Pursuit but it's double time
Speed is doubled, and all tiles have another tile with the same angle appended after it.
- Inverse: Speed is halved, and every pair of tiles is merged. The angle of the tile before when the curse ends is halved.

### ◆10-X - Butterfly Planet but it's a bit off
Any non-onbeat sections have their angles altered greatly. Go nuts!
- Inverse: Non-onbeat sections are confined to a specific rhythm.

### ◆11-X - Heracles but inside angles have outside midspins
Inside-angled tiles have a midspin on their outside side, intersecting the angle's bisector.

### ◆12-X - Artificial Chariot but all non-horizontal segments are symmetrical
Any track segments not comprised of horizontal tiles are altered such that they are symmetrical along the horizontal or vertical axes, or 180-degree rotation.

## Extra Island
If you think about it, each curse has a trigger and an effect.
For example, ◆1-X has a trigger of "outside-angled tile" and an effect of "add a 2-beat pause"
(note that triggers and effects are up to interpretation).
The curses in this section change the trigger/effect of other curses.

### ◆XF-X - Third Wave Flip Flop but the attached curse's effect is applied on every tile
Self-explanatory.
### ◆XC-X - Credits but the attached curse can't change any gameplay icons
The attached curse may do this by either altering the chart or removing any wrong icons after its effect.
### ◆XH-X - Final Hope but the attached curse can't change the rhythm
This is done with speed changes.
The attached curse may still alter the rhythm in a way that still fits with the song.
